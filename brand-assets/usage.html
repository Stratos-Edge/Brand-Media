Stratos Edge.

One mark in three forms. Use the fullest form that fits the space: the lockup, then the wordmark, then the S on its own.

Three forms

The lockup is the mark beside the wordmark. The wordmark is STRATOS and EDGE with the three dots between them. The S is the mark alone, for tight spaces and icons.

On ink

On a dark background the letters are white and the bottom piece of the mark turns white. Coral and steel stay as they are.

The S as an icon

Where one fixed square image stands for us (favicon, profile picture, app icon) the S sits on its canvas. The canvas is light or dark to match the surface. favicon.svg carries both and follows the system theme.

Where each file goes

The icon on the canvas when the surface is not ours. The bare mark on our own pages, where the background is already ours.

The mark moves

The S can rest as three stacked dots and become the S, or sit as the S and open into the dots while the pointer is over it. It should always end on the S.

Colours

Top to bottom, always in this order: coral for people, steel for productivity, ink for automation. The same three dots divide the wordmark.

Space and size

Round the wordmark and the lockup keep clear space equal to the height of the capitals. Round the bare mark keep one dot; the mark files already include it. On the canvas the S works down to 16 px; bare, keep it at least 20 px tall. The wordmark should not go below 14 px of cap height.

Don't

  • Draw an outline round the pieces.
  • Change, swap or add colours.
  • Rotate, stretch or re-space the pieces or the letters.
  • Put the bare mark on a photo or a mid-tone. Use the canvas.
  • Set the wordmark in another typeface, or bring back the line between the words.
